# Compliance

Read before writing claims, testimonials or AI-generated people, and before
any ad goes to review. This is practical guidance, not legal advice; when a
claim is borderline, say so and suggest the person checks it.

## Testimonials And AI People

- No fake reviews or testimonials. In the US the FTC's rule on fake reviews and
  testimonials (announced 14 August 2024) bans testimonials that misrepresent
  who gave them or that the person had the experience, including AI-generated
  "customers". The UK, EU and Australia have comparable consumer law.
- An AI actor may demonstrate or explain a product. It must not present itself
  as a real customer describing an experience that didn't happen.
- Real customer quotes need the customer's permission and must be typical, or
  say they are not.
- No celebrity look-alikes, and no copies of real creators or customers
  without written permission.
- Use the platform's AI disclosure label where Meta's current policy asks for
  it (realistic AI-generated or altered people, events or sounds).

## Claims

- Results, numbers, rankings, "clinically proven", health, income and
  "guaranteed" claims need evidence on file. If the brief doesn't list it,
  it's `[PROOF NEEDED]`.
- Before/after imagery and "you" statements about personal attributes
  ("Are you overweight?", "your debt") are common rejection reasons on Meta.
  Talk about the product and the situation, not the viewer's traits.
- Comparisons must be fair and checkable. Don't name competitors unless the
  person has approved it and the comparison is accurate.

## Competitor Material

- Studying the public Ad Library is what it is for. Copying another brand's
  words, footage, images or trademarks is not; run `npm run creative:overlap`
  on new copy.
- If a third-party scraper is used, the person is responsible for its terms
  and the platform's.

## Special Ad Categories

Ads about credit, employment, housing, or social issues, elections and politics
must be declared in Meta's special ad category, which limits targeting. Ask
before building one; never work around it.

## When An Ad Is Rejected

Read the reason, rewrite the copy or image text inside the brief's claims, and
resubmit as a new version (`v2`). Don't appeal or resubmit unchanged without the
person's yes.
